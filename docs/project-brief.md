# Edunura Community Engagement Platform

## Project Brief

Edunura is building a community-driven student engagement platform designed to transform educational outreach events into measurable, scalable, and high-impact experiences for children and parents.

The platform powers interactive learning festivals conducted across residential communities, schools, and public spaces where children participate in educational games, skill-based activities, and fun learning challenges through multiple themed stalls.

Each participating child receives a unique event identity, enabling seamless participation tracking across all activity zones. Volunteers at each stall evaluate participation, assign performance grades, and contribute toward a personalized event report card generated at the final engagement counter.

The platform is designed not only to manage events operationally, but also to create a strong data-driven ecosystem around:

* student participation,
* parent engagement,
* community activation,
* educational analytics,
* and enrollment conversion opportunities.

# How the Platform Works

## 1. Student Registration

Children are registered at the event and assigned:

* unique roll numbers,
* participation identity,
* and event tracking access.

## 2. Activity Participation

Students visit multiple educational stalls where volunteers evaluate:

* participation,
* creativity,
* problem-solving,
* communication,
* and learning abilities.

## 3. Performance Tracking

Each stall records:

* scores,
* grades,
* remarks,
* completion status,
* and participation behavior.

## 4. Final Report Generation

At the final engagement desk:

* personalized report cards are generated,
* participation summaries are printed,
* rewards are distributed,
* and parents are introduced to Edunura learning programs.
